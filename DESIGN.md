# Journal26 visual direction

The owner writes at night on a phone and reviews during the day on a laptop: retain adaptive light and dark themes, with a warm paper writing surface in daylight and a muted ink surface at night.

Keep Hanken Grotesk for controls and Newsreader for personal writing. Retain the existing ink-blue accent and established semantic success, warning and loss colors. Introduce a restrained warm paper tint for the writing invitation, with a small illustrated flower as a writing-progress motif. New colors use OKLCH.

Writing leads the Today hierarchy; seven labelled days show participation. The full challenge board remains available below. Controls use existing buttons and focus rings. New touch targets are at least 44px. Motion is a short state-feedback transform, with a reduced-motion override. No new dependencies or external image services.
