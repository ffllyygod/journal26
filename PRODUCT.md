# Product

## Register
product

## Users
A single owner returning on a phone or laptop to a private 60-day journal, often tired at the end of the day. Existing context and constraints are recorded in docs/PROJECT_STATUS.md.

## Product Purpose
Make a small daily reflection easy enough to return to. Support habits, planning, career and trading without making those activities prerequisites for writing.

## Brand Personality
Warm, playful, encouraging. The owner explicitly requested a more joyful, interactive experience. Honest entries and returning after gaps deserve recognition.

## Anti-references
Avoid guilt-driven streak pressure, compulsory productivity checklists before writing, fake rewards, and decorative dashboards that bury the journal.

## Design Principles
- One line is a worthwhile visit.
- Writing progress and full routine completion are different achievements.
- Offer prompts without writing on the owner's behalf.
- Make feedback truthful, calm and easy to dismiss by moving on.
- Preserve private data, existing sync and export paths.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Support keyboard use, visible focus, labelled controls, phone-sized touch targets, dark mode and reduced motion. Do not rely on color alone for progress.
